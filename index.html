How to play

  • Left thumb: drag anywhere on the left side to move. Push all the way forward to sprint.
  • Right thumb: drag on the right side to look. Hold the bullet button to fire — you can keep dragging it to aim while shooting.
  • Crosshair-circle = aim down sights / sniper scope. Tap a weapon card to swap guns. Stripes button reloads.
  • Grenade explodes; ink drop makes a cloud that blocks NPC vision.
  • Grab + health, shield and ammo doodles on the map.
  • Every kill = 1 point. First to 20 wins. Earn XP for kills, headshots, streaks and placing — level up to unlock faces, ink colours and titles.
  • Desktop: WASD, mouse, Space, C, R, Q, G, F, right-click.
